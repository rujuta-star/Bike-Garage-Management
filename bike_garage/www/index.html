Features

Everything a service garage keeps track of

Purpose-built modules that follow the way your workshop already works.

Customer Management

Keep contact details and service history for every customer organized in one place.

Motorcycle Management

Maintain bike details and registration records linked to their owners.

Job Card Management

Track service work from intake to delivery, with clear job status for every bike.

Spare-Parts Inventory

Review the parts you carry and see available stock before you quote a job.

Sales and Billing

Organize sales and invoice-related workflows alongside the jobs they belong to.

Expense Tracking

Record and review business expenses so day-to-day costs never get lost.

Reports and Analytics

Understand revenue, costs and workshop activity through clear reports.

Outstanding Payments

Review unpaid and partially paid records and follow up with confidence.

How it works

Three steps to an organized garage

Getting started is simple. Every workspace is set up for one garage.

  1. STEP 01

    Request access

    Tell us about your garage and request your own workspace.

  2. STEP 02

    Get approved

    Your garage account is reviewed and approved before you can sign in.

  3. STEP 03

    Get organized

    Add customers, bikes, service jobs and business records in one place.
