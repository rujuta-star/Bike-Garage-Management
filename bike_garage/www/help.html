Getting started

Help / Support

Follow the steps below to request a garage workspace, set up your account, and sign in.

1. Request garage access

While signed out, open the Request Access form and enter the garage name, owner's name, work email, and phone number. Address, city, state, and PIN code are optional. Submitting the form creates a pending application only; it does not create an account or membership. The form displays a generic acknowledgement and does not send an email at this stage.

2. Wait for administrator review

A Bike Garage administrator reviews the application. Until it is approved and registration is completed, there is no garage owner login created by the request form. If you already have an account, a new access request for that same email will not create a duplicate application.

3. Complete account setup

After approval, the administrator can send a separate registration link to the email on the application when outgoing email is configured. The link expires after 48 hours. Follow it to set a password and complete owner registration. If no email arrives, contact your garage administrator using their existing support channel.

4. Sign in and open your workspace

Go to Sign In and use the account credentials you created. The workspace is available when the account has a valid active garage membership. Your permitted destination is selected by the application after authentication.

Password recovery

Use the password-recovery link on the native Frappe login page and follow its instructions. If you cannot access the email on your account, ask your garage administrator for help.

Contact support

For workspace access or garage-specific questions, contact your garage administrator. You can also email the application contact listed on the Contact Us page with your garage name and a description; do not include your password or authentication codes.
